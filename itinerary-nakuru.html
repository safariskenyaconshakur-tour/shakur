Lake Nakuru Safari

2 Days Lake Nakuru Safari

Visit the beautiful Lake Nakuru National Park famous for flamingos, rhinos and stunning views of the Great Rift Valley.

Day 1 – Nairobi to Lake Nakuru

Morning departure from Nairobi driving through the Great Rift Valley. Arrive at Lake Nakuru National Park and begin a game drive searching for rhinos, lions, giraffes and flamingos.

Day 2 – Morning Game Drive and Return

Enjoy an early morning game drive when wildlife is most active. After breakfast depart for Nairobi arriving in the afternoon.
