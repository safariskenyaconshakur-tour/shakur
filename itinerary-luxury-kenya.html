Luxury Kenya Safari

6 Days Luxury Kenya Safari

Experience the best of Kenya with this luxury safari adventure. Stay in comfortable lodges while exploring famous wildlife parks including Masai Mara, Lake Nakuru and Amboseli.

Day 1 – Nairobi to Lake Nakuru

Morning pickup from Nairobi and drive through the Great Rift Valley to Lake Nakuru National Park. Afternoon game drive searching for rhinos, flamingos and other wildlife.

Day 2 – Lake Nakuru to Masai Mara

After breakfast depart for Masai Mara National Reserve. Afternoon game drive exploring the reserve.

Day 3 – Full Day Masai Mara Safari

Enjoy a full day game drive searching for lions, elephants, buffalo, cheetahs and other wildlife.

Day 4 – Masai Mara to Amboseli

Travel to Amboseli National Park famous for its large elephant herds and stunning views of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Day 5 – Full Day Amboseli Safari

Morning and afternoon game drives with spectacular wildlife photography opportunities.

Day 6 – Return to Nairobi

After breakfast depart for Nairobi arriving in the afternoon.
