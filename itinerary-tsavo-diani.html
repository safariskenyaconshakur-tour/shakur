Tsavo Safari & Diani Beach

5 Days Tsavo Safari & Diani Beach Holiday

Enjoy the perfect Kenya holiday combining wildlife safari and relaxing beach time. Explore Tsavo East National Park and then unwind on the beautiful white sands of Diani Beach.

Day 1 – Mombasa / Diani to Tsavo East

Early morning pickup from your beach hotel and drive to Tsavo East National Park. Enjoy a game drive searching for elephants, lions and giraffes.

Day 2 – Full Day Tsavo Safari

Morning and afternoon game drives exploring the vast savannah of Tsavo East and visiting famous spots like Aruba Dam.

Day 3 – Tsavo to Diani Beach

After breakfast depart for Diani Beach arriving in the afternoon for relaxation at your beach resort.

Day 4 – Beach Relaxation

Enjoy swimming, snorkeling, dhow sailing or relaxing on the beautiful white sand beaches of Diani.

Day 5 – Departure

Breakfast at the hotel and transfer to Mombasa airport or continue your beach holiday.
