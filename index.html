🐘 3 Days Masai Mara Safari

Day 1 – Nairobi to Masai Mara

Pick up from your hotel in Nairobi and drive to Masai Mara. Enjoy scenic views of the Great Rift Valley. Arrive in time for lunch and afternoon game drive.

Day 2 – Full Day Safari

Spend the whole day exploring Masai Mara. Look for lions, elephants, cheetahs, giraffes and zebras. Picnic lunch inside the park.

Day 3 – Morning Safari & Return

Early morning game drive then breakfast at the lodge. Depart for Nairobi arriving in the afternoon.

🦁 3 Days Amboseli Safari

Day 1 – Nairobi to Amboseli

Pick up from Nairobi and drive to Amboseli National Park. Arrive in time for lunch and enjoy an afternoon game drive with views of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Day 2 – Full Day Game Drive

Spend the day exploring Amboseli famous for large elephant herds. Enjoy incredible wildlife viewing and photography with Mount Kilimanjaro in the background.

Day 3 – Morning Safari & Return

Early morning game drive then breakfast at the lodge before returning to Nairobi in the afternoon.
