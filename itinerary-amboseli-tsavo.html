Amboseli & Tsavo Safari

4 Days Amboseli & Tsavo Safari

Enjoy an unforgettable safari exploring two of Kenya’s most famous parks. See large elephant herds in Amboseli with breathtaking views of Mount Kilimanjaro, then discover the wild landscapes of Tsavo East with its famous red elephants.

Day 1 – Nairobi / Mombasa to Amboseli

Morning pickup and drive to Amboseli National Park. Afternoon game drive with views of elephants and Mount Kilimanjaro.

Day 2 – Full Day Amboseli Safari

Enjoy morning and afternoon game drives searching for lions, giraffes, zebras and large elephant herds.

Day 3 – Amboseli to Tsavo East

After breakfast depart for Tsavo East National Park. Afternoon game drive exploring the park’s famous red elephants and vast savannah.

Day 4 – Morning Safari and Return

Early morning game drive followed by breakfast before returning to Nairobi or the coast.
