Marafa Hell's Kitchen

Marafa Canyon Sunset Excursion

Discover the breathtaking natural canyon of Marafa, also known as Hell’s Kitchen. The colorful sandstone gorges change color throughout the day, creating one of the most spectacular landscapes on the Kenya coast.

Tour Highlights

✔ Explore the famous Marafa Canyon
✔ Amazing sunset photography
✔ Guided walk through the sandstone formations
✔ Learn the local legends of the canyon

Tour Duration

Half Day Excursion (Afternoon – Sunset Tour)

Location

About 40 km from Watamu.
