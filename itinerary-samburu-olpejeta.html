Samburu & Ol Pejeta Safari

4 Days Samburu & Ol Pejeta Safari

Explore the wild beauty of northern Kenya with this unique safari. Visit Samburu National Reserve to see rare wildlife and continue to Ol Pejeta Conservancy, one of Africa’s leading wildlife conservation areas.

Day 1 – Nairobi to Samburu

Morning pickup from Nairobi and drive north through the scenic Kenyan highlands to Samburu National Reserve. Afternoon game drive.

Day 2 – Full Day Samburu Safari

Enjoy game drives searching for Samburu’s special wildlife including Grevy’s zebra, Somali ostrich, reticulated giraffe and lions.

Day 3 – Samburu to Ol Pejeta

After breakfast depart for Ol Pejeta Conservancy. Visit the rhino sanctuary and see the last northern white rhinos.

Day 4 – Return to Nairobi

Morning game drive before returning to Nairobi in the afternoon.
