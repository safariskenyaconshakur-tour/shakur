Masai Mara & Lake Nakuru Safari

4 Days Masai Mara & Lake Nakuru Safari

This exciting safari combines two of Kenya’s most famous wildlife destinations. Explore the incredible Big Five in the Masai Mara and visit Lake Nakuru National Park, famous for flamingos and rhinos.

Day 1 – Nairobi to Masai Mara

Morning pickup from Nairobi and drive through the Great Rift Valley to Masai Mara National Reserve. Afternoon game drive.

Day 2 – Full Day Masai Mara Safari

Spend the day exploring the reserve searching for lions, elephants, buffalo, leopards and cheetahs.

Day 3 – Masai Mara to Lake Nakuru

After breakfast depart for Lake Nakuru National Park. Afternoon game drive searching for rhinos and flamingos.

Day 4 – Return to Nairobi

Morning game drive before returning to Nairobi in the afternoon.
