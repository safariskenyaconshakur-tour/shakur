Mida Creek Sunset Tour

Mida Creek Mangrove & Sunset Excursion

Enjoy a peaceful tour to Mida Creek, a beautiful mangrove lagoon near Watamu. Walk along the famous wooden boardwalk through the mangrove forest and enjoy one of the most beautiful sunsets on the Kenya coast.

Tour Highlights

✔ Walk along the Mida Creek boardwalk
✔ Beautiful sunset views
✔ Bird watching and mangrove forest
✔ Relaxing nature experience

Tour Duration

Half Day Excursion (Afternoon – Sunset)

Location

Mida Creek, near Watamu.
