Masai Mara Safari

3 Days Masai Mara Safari

Experience one of Africa’s most famous safari destinations in the :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}. This tour offers incredible opportunities to see the Big Five, beautiful landscapes, and unforgettable wildlife encounters.

Safari Highlights

✔ Big Five wildlife viewing (lion, elephant, buffalo, leopard, rhino)
✔ Professional safari guide
✔ Scenic drive through the Great Rift Valley
✔ Optional Maasai village visit

Day 1 – Arrival & Transfer

Pick up from your hotel or airport in Nairobi, Mombasa or Watamu. Drive or fly to Masai Mara. Upon arrival, check in to your lodge and enjoy an afternoon game drive searching for wildlife.

Day 2 – Full Day Game Drive

Spend the whole day exploring Masai Mara with morning and afternoon game drives. Spot lions, elephants, giraffes, zebras and many other animals in their natural habitat.

Day 3 – Morning Safari & Return

Enjoy an early morning game drive followed by breakfast. Later depart and travel back to your hotel or airport.
