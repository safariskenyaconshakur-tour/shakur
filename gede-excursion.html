Gede Ruins Tour

Half Day Gede Ruins Excursion

Discover the mysterious ruins of the ancient Swahili town of Gede, hidden deep in the coastal forest near Watamu. The site dates back to the 12th century and includes mosques, palaces and houses built with coral stone.

Tour Highlights

✔ Explore ancient Swahili ruins
✔ See old mosques and palace remains
✔ Walk through the forest full of monkeys
✔ Learn about Swahili culture and history

Tour Duration

Half Day Excursion (3–4 hours)

Location

Located about 7 km from Watamu.
