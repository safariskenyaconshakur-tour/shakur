Tsavo East Safari

2 Days Tsavo East Safari

Explore one of Kenya’s largest national parks famous for its red elephants, lions, giraffes and stunning landscapes. Tsavo East offers a true African wilderness experience.

Day 1 – Mombasa / Diani to Tsavo East

Early morning pickup from your hotel and drive to Tsavo East National Park. Enter through Bachuma Gate and begin your first exciting game drive. Enjoy lunch at the lodge followed by an afternoon game drive.

Day 2 – Morning Safari and Return

Early morning game drive when wildlife is most active. After breakfast, continue a short game drive before exiting the park and returning to your hotel.
