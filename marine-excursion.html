Watamu Marine Park Snorkeling Excursion

Enjoy an unforgettable boat trip to Watamu Marine National Park, one of the most beautiful marine parks in Kenya. Discover colorful coral reefs, tropical fish and crystal clear waters.

Tour Highlights

✔ Snorkeling in coral reefs
✔ Dolphin spotting (often seen in the morning)
✔ Traditional dhow boat experience
✔ Relax on the beautiful sandbanks

Tour Duration

Half Day Excursion (Morning Boat Trip)

Location

Watamu Marine National Park – Indian Ocean.
