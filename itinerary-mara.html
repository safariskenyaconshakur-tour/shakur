Masai Mara Safari

3 Days Masai Mara Safari

Experience an unforgettable wildlife safari in the famous Masai Mara. See lions, elephants, cheetahs and the Big Five in one of Africa’s most famous reserves.

Day 1 – Nairobi to Masai Mara

Pick up from Nairobi hotel or airport. Drive through the Great Rift Valley to the Masai Mara. Afternoon game drive.

Day 2 – Full Day Game Drive

Morning and afternoon game drives searching for wildlife including lions, elephants, buffalo and cheetahs.

Day 3 – Return to Nairobi

Morning breakfast and drive back to Nairobi with optional stops for photos.
