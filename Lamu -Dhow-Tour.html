Lamu Dhow Sunset Tour

Lamu Dhow Sunset Experience

Enjoy a magical evening sailing on a traditional dhow along the beautiful waters of. This relaxing cruise offers stunning sunset views, peaceful ocean breeze, and a true Swahili coastal experience.

Tour Highlights

✔ Traditional wooden dhow sailing
✔ Spectacular sunset views over the Indian Ocean
✔ Relaxing and romantic atmosphere
✔ Swahili snacks and refreshments (optional)
✔ Perfect for couples, families, and groups

Tour Program

Departure: Late afternoon (around 4:30 PM)

Board your traditional dhow and sail through the calm waters surrounding Lamu. Enjoy the gentle breeze as the sun begins to set over the ocean.

Relax on board as you watch the sky change colors from blue to orange and red. You may enjoy light snacks, fresh coconut, or drinks while listening to the sounds of the sea.

Return: After sunset (around 6:30 PM – 7:00 PM)

What’s Included

✔ Dhow boat ride
✔ Local guide / captain
✔ Life jackets
✔ Refreshments (depending on package)

Not Included

✘ Transport to Lamu (can be arranged)
✘ Personal expenses
✘ Tips

Optional Add-ons

✔ Seafood dinner on the dhow
✔ Private dhow booking
✔ Full day Lamu excursion (Old Town + dhow)
