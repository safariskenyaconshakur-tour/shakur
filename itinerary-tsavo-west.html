Tsavo West Safari

3 Days Tsavo West Safari

Experience the rugged beauty of Tsavo West National Park. This safari takes you through volcanic landscapes, natural springs and rich wildlife habitats where elephants, lions and leopards roam freely.

Day 1 – Nairobi / Mombasa to Tsavo West

Morning pickup and drive to Tsavo West National Park. Enter the park for your first exciting game drive before checking into the lodge.

Day 2 – Full Day Game Drive

Explore the park including a visit to the famous Mzima Springs, where crystal clear water attracts hippos and crocodiles. Enjoy spectacular scenery and wildlife viewing.

Day 3 – Morning Safari and Return

After breakfast enjoy a short game drive before departing for Nairobi or Mombasa.
