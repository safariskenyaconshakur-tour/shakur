Samburu Safari

3 Days Samburu Safari

Discover the unique wildlife of northern Kenya in Samburu National Reserve. This beautiful semi-arid park is home to rare animals known as the Samburu Special Five.

Day 1 – Nairobi to Samburu

Morning pickup from Nairobi and drive north through scenic landscapes to Samburu. Arrive in time for lunch followed by an afternoon game drive along the Ewaso Nyiro River.

Day 2 – Full Day Game Drive

Enjoy a full day exploring the reserve searching for elephants, lions, leopards, Grevy’s zebra and reticulated giraffes.

Day 3 – Return to Nairobi

After breakfast, enjoy a short morning game drive before departing back to Nairobi with scenic stops along the way.
