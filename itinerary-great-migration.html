Great Migration Safari

4 Days Great Migration Masai Mara Safari

Witness one of the greatest wildlife spectacles on earth — the Great Wildebeest Migration in the Masai Mara. Millions of wildebeest and zebras cross the plains and rivers while predators follow closely behind.

Day 1 – Nairobi to Masai Mara

Morning pickup from Nairobi and scenic drive through the Great Rift Valley to Masai Mara National Reserve. Afternoon game drive.

Day 2 – Migration Safari

Full day game drive following the wildebeest migration and searching for lions, cheetahs and crocodiles at the river crossings.

Day 3 – Another Full Day Safari

Continue exploring the Masai Mara and enjoy incredible wildlife photography opportunities.

Day 4 – Return to Nairobi

Morning breakfast and drive back to Nairobi with stops for scenic views along the way.
