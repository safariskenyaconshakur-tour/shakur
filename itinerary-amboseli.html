Amboseli Safari

3 Days Amboseli Safari

Experience breathtaking wildlife views in the famous Amboseli ecosystem. Amboseli is known for large elephant herds and spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Day 1 – Nairobi to Amboseli

Pick up from your Nairobi hotel or airport and drive to Amboseli National Park. Afternoon game drive exploring the park.

Day 2 – Full Day Game Drive

Spend the day searching for elephants, lions, cheetahs and many bird species with the beautiful Mount Kilimanjaro in the background.

Day 3 – Return to Nairobi

Morning game drive and breakfast before returning to Nairobi.
