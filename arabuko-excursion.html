Arabuko Sokoke Forest Nature Tour

Discover the incredible biodiversity of Arabuko Sokoke Forest, East Africa’s largest remaining coastal forest. This tour is perfect for nature lovers and bird watchers.

Tour Highlights

✔ Guided forest nature walk
✔ Bird watching (over 200 species)
✔ Rare butterflies and wildlife
✔ Visit local conservation projects

Tour Duration

Half Day or Full Day Excursion

Location

Arabuko Sokoke Forest Reserve near Watamu.
